---
name: Guided photo route
description: The shop’s physical walk-around order and the reliability rules that keep captures attached to the intended guided angle.
---

Use the guided sequence **exterior → wheels/tires → interior**, but keep the live camera free of angle/title prompts.

**Why:** A hidden sequence that placed interior before wheels silently mislabeled correctly taken photos when an inspector followed the shop’s physical route. Async review, persistence, and uploads can also outlive the render that started a capture. The shop explicitly prefers an uncluttered capture view without angle suggestions.

**How to apply:** Reserve the slot at shutter time and carry that immutable target through quality review and persistence, without displaying it in the live viewfinder. Order retakes by capture generation, never upload arrival time, so an older delayed request cannot overwrite or roll back a newer image.
